<h2 style="color:#0f6dbf">Course Overview</h2>
This course covers all the basic building blocks necessary to learn and master DevOps. So, stop thinking and just dive into it. However, before you dive in, I have a few points to share.

Think of this course as building blocks or Lego pieces. You join multiple pieces together to form a structure. Initially, you will be learning basic skills such as Linux, virtualization, networking, and scripting to form that structure.

You can think of this structure like the chassis of a car. On this strong chassis, you will fit in the engine, wheels, and the body of the car. Similarly, on your strong chassis, you will integrate AWS, Jenkins, Ansible, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and many other DevOps tools.

Can there really be a car without a chassis? You see where I am going with this? Once you make your foundational choices strong, you will gain the confidence to delve deep into DevOps. And that is the key.

<h2 style="color:#0f6dbf">Learning Approach</h2>
This is a very practical course. You are going to learn by doing. Do not skip or jump any lecture. Take your time and do not rush. Initially, build rapport with the course and the lectures you are doing.

Pause, rewind, or slow the speed of the video if required. I promise you will develop skills that will stay with you for a lifetime.

So, what are you waiting for? Buckle up and get ready for the exciting journey of DevOps.

<h2 style="color:#0f6dbf">Key Takeaways</h2>

- DevOps learning is structured like building blocks, starting with foundational skills.
- Basic skills include Linux, virtualization, networking, and scripting.
- These foundational skills form a strong chassis to support advanced DevOps tools like AWS, Jenkins, Ansible, Kubernetes, and CI/CD.
- The course emphasizes practical, hands-on learning with a steady, unhurried approach.
