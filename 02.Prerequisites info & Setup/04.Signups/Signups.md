# Signups
## Creating a GitHub Account
We will start by creating an account on GitHub. Open your browser and navigate to github.com. The signup process is straightforward: provide a username, email address, and password, then click sign up. After this, confirm your email address from your inbox to complete the registration and log in to github.com.

Fill in your correct details and proceed with the signup. You may be asked to complete a verification step, such as identifying an image (e.g., Spiral Galaxy). Once verified, you can opt out of receiving offers or announcements and select the free plan, which is sufficient for our projects.

After signing up, GitHub will ask about your primary work type. You can select options such as teacher or student. For example, select "teacher" or "software engineer". Next, indicate your programming experience level and your intended use of GitHub, such as learning Git and GitHub or hosting projects. Complete the setup and verify your email address from your inbox.

Once your email is verified, your GitHub account setup is complete. We will use this account later in our projects.

## Purchasing a Domain
Next, we will discuss purchasing a low-cost domain. This domain is necessary for real-time or production use cases. While purchasing a domain is recommended, you may skip these steps if you prefer not to buy one at this time.

Sign in to GoDaddy or create an account if you do not have one. The account creation process is simple: click "create an account," fill in the details, and log in with your credentials.

After logging in, search for a domain name. Common domain names with extensions like .com or .in can be expensive, especially if the name is common (e.g., myDevOps or myprojects). To save costs, consider unique names such as "hkhinfo" with the extension .xyz.

For example, searching for "hkhinfo.xyz" shows a price of 169 INR, whereas "hkhinfo.com" is 139 INR but may cost more at checkout. Select the desired domain and reduce the registration period to one year to minimize costs. Proceed to the cart.

At checkout, decline additional offers such as full domain protection or website hosting from GoDaddy, as we will deploy our own website. Ensure only free privacy protection is selected. Confirm the domain and price before proceeding to payment.

If you see a higher price, verify that the domain extension is .xyz and the registration period is set to one year. You may choose other extensions like .com or .in, but these will incur additional costs. Complete the purchase by filling in your details and submitting payment.

If you are not comfortable purchasing a domain now, you can skip these steps or do it later when you want to perform production use cases. However, purchasing a domain is strongly recommended for practical experience.

## Creating a Docker Hub Account
Next, create an account on Docker Hub. Navigate to hub.docker.com and click sign up. Provide a username, email address, and password, then complete the signup process by verifying you are not a robot.

Select the free plan, which is sufficient for our needs. Verify your email address to complete the Docker Hub account setup. We will use this account to store Docker images for our projects.

## Creating a Sonar Cloud Account
Finally, create an account on Sonar Cloud. Before proceeding, ensure you are logged into your GitHub account in the same browser, as we will sign up using GitHub credentials.

Open https://sonarcloud.io or search for Sonar Cloud and navigate to the site. Click sign up and select the GitHub option. Authorize Sonar Cloud to access your GitHub account to complete the signup process.

With this, the signup process is complete. We now have accounts on GitHub, Docker Hub, and Sonar Cloud, as well as a purchased domain. We have also configured the organization and project in Sonar Cloud, which we will use in our projects.

## Key Takeaways
- Created accounts on GitHub, Docker Hub, and Sonar Cloud to support development and deployment workflows.
- Purchased a low-cost domain from GoDaddy for real-time and production use cases.
- Verified email addresses and selected appropriate free plans for all services.
- Emphasized the importance of having a domain for production scenarios but allowed flexibility to skip if not comfortable.
